# Investigation timeline

The case workspace timeline combines recorded dates from evidence and analyst activity:
- Source publication time.
- Source/media metadata timestamp.
- Time evidence was added to the case.
- Analyst notes.
- Investigation workflow transitions.
- Task deadlines and task audit events.
- Video keyframe offsets relative to their clip.

The timeline distinguishes source timestamps from Calibrex collection time. Video offsets are not treated as absolute dates. Entries with unknown dates remain visible after dated events rather than being assigned invented timestamps. Chronological order does not establish causality, and source-provided dates retain their original provenance and timezone caveats.
