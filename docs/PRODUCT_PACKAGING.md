# Calibrex OSINT Studio — Product Packaging

This packaging is a proposed commercial structure. Prices must be validated against operating cost, AI consumption, support burden and customer willingness to pay before publication.

## Customer segments

1. Corporate security, resilience and risk teams.
2. Supply chain, shipping, manufacturing, energy and infrastructure operators.
3. Intelligence, due-diligence and risk consultancies.
4. Media research and investigative teams.
5. Strategic affairs and research organisations.

## Proposed packages

| Package | Buyer | Scope | Commercial model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Analyst | Independent analyst | One named user, core monitoring and research | Per-seat monthly/annual |
| Professional | Small specialist team | Team seats, shared monitoring configuration after workspace capability is released | Per-seat + usage |
| Enterprise | Corporate risk/security function | Organisation workspace, governance, audit and agreed integrations | Annual contract |
| Institutional | Large research or public-interest institution | Custom deployment, onboarding and support | Custom contract |

Do not sell shared team workspaces as available until the organisation tenancy work in COMMERCIAL_READINESS.md is complete.

## Capability groups

- Monitor: public news and social-source collection, regional wires, watchlists, source health.
- Detect: chatter themes, emerging event candidates, configured alert rules and explainable priority.
- Investigate: case workspace, entity candidates, relationships, evidence ledger and history.
- Understand: market and macro indicators, public infrastructure and hazard signals.
- Brief: AI-assisted synthesis, source-linked reports and exports.

## Usage boundaries

- Public source availability, rate limits, publisher controls and indexing delays affect coverage.
- Social posts are unverified leads unless independently corroborated.
- Corroboration measures independent coverage signals, not truth.
- Market data may be delayed and must not be represented as an execution-grade trading feed.
- AI output requires analyst review and source verification.
- X API collection is not included; no X bearer token is required.
- No new third-party API credentials are required for the credential-free public-source baseline.

## Pricing approval checklist

Before publishing prices, model:
- Active seats and concurrent users.
- Ingestion volume, storage and retention.
- AI tokens and provider costs by workflow.
- Report/export volume.
- Support hours and onboarding effort.
- Hosting, database, backups and observability.
- Taxes, payment charges and annual contract discounts.

Avoid unlimited-use promises until load and unit-economics tests support them.
