# Calibrex Quickstart — Your first hour

*A getting-started guide for new users.*

Calibrex OSINT Studio watches world events from public sources and helps you
verify and report them. This takes you from your first sign-in to your first
dispatch — no prior training needed.

## Five steps

1. **Request access, then wait for activation.** On the sign-in page choose
   *Request access* and enter your details. Your account starts as **pending**;
   an administrator has to approve you. Until then you'll see an
   "awaiting activation" screen with a contact. Once approved, sign in normally.

2. **Get your bearings on the Dashboard.** Markers are events, grouped by place
   and coloured by severity (critical / high / low). The feed updates every five
   minutes; new items are marked **NEW**. Click any item, then use **Read** for
   the full article and **Visual intel** for its photos, video and satellite views.

3. **Research a topic and verify it.** Open **Intelligence Research** and search
   any place, group, vessel or event. Click **Verify** on a report to see how
   widely it's confirmed — three or more independent outlets means corroborated.
   **Pin** the reports you want to keep.

4. **Compile a report.** In **Report Generator**, pinned reports become an
   editable draft with every source attached. Run a corroboration audit, then
   format and export from **Dispatch Studio**. Saved reports land in **Report History**.

5. **Set a watchlist so the news finds you.** In **Watchlists**, add the terms
   you track. Calibrex checks every new report against them and alerts you from
   the bell in the header when one matches.

## Every screen at a glance

| Screen | What it does |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Live map, wire feed and headline counts. |
| Crisis Monitor | Conflict view plus earthquake and disaster overlays. |
| Visual Intel | All photos, video and satellite imagery in one gallery. |
| Research | Live news search, verification and pinning. |
| Report Generator | Build a sourced brief from pinned reports. |
| Dispatch Studio | Format and export the finished report. |
| Watchlists | Track terms and get in-app alerts. |
| Trends | Daily volume by topic, severity and place. |
| OSINT Tools | A curated toolkit plus built-in domain/IP recon. |
| Subject Lookup | Gated phone/username lookups (only if your provider enables it). |
| Settings | Connect AI, text size, and your alert preferences. |

## Optional: connect your own AI

In **Settings → AI Connection** you can link your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini
account. AI buttons then appear to draft reports, summarise searches and explain
verification — always from the sources on your screen. It's optional, and
Calibrex works fully without it.

## Tips

- **Treat leads as leads.** Social posts are flagged unverified, and images can be old or recycled. Confirm before you publish.
- **Use Read, not just the headline.** The full article opens inside Calibrex.
- **Comfortable text is on** by default (Settings → Display); turn on larger text there too.
- **Works on your phone** — a bottom nav bar gives you Home, Research, Watch and Report.
