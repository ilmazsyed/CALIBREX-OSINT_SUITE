# Calibrex Quickstart — Your first hour

*A getting-started guide for new users.*

Calibrex OSINT Studio watches world events from public sources and helps you
verify and report them. This takes you from your first sign-in to your first
dispatch — no prior training needed.

## Six steps

1. **Request access, then wait for activation.** On the sign-in page choose
   *Request access* and enter your details. Your account starts as **pending**;
   an administrator has to approve you. Until then you'll see an
   "awaiting activation" screen with a contact. Once approved, sign in normally.

2. **Start on Command.** Command is your home screen: a composite risk index and
   a live band for every monitor. Use the **Global / India / World** toggle to
   re-scope the whole picture, and fire a **Rapid Brief** straight from the top
   when something needs writing up.

3. **Work the Watch boards.** The **Watch** strip links every live board —
   Security, Markets, Business, Government, Politics, Signals, Earth, Conflict and
   Maritime. Markers are events, grouped by place and coloured by severity
   (critical / high / low). Feeds refresh every five minutes; new items are marked
   **NEW**. Click any item, then **Read** for the full article and
   **Visual intel** for its photos, video and satellite views.

4. **Research, verify and pivot.** Open **Intelligence Research** and search any
   place, group, vessel or event. Click **Verify** to see how widely it's
   confirmed — three or more independent outlets means corroborated. Take a
   domain, IP, email or name into **Link Analysis** to map connected
   infrastructure, and screen names in **Sanctions Screening**. **Pin** the
   reports you want to keep.

5. **Organise, then compile.** In the **Workbench**, add items — plus threats
   from the map — into a **Case**; Cases keep your notes and sources together and
   can export a court-ready evidence report with SHA-256 integrity hashes. In
   **Reports**, pinned reports become an editable, fully sourced draft you can
   audit, export and archive. Rapid Briefs land in **Briefs**.

6. **Set alerts and secure your account.** In **Watchlists**, add the terms you
   track — Calibrex checks every new report against them and alerts you from the
   bell in the header. In **Settings → Alert Delivery**, push high-severity alerts
   to Telegram or a webhook (optionally geofenced). Also in Settings: connect your
   own AI, edit your profile, and turn on **two-factor sign-in**.

## Every screen at a glance

| Screen | What it does |
|---|---|
| Command | Composite risk index + a band per monitor, scoped Global / India / World. |
| Security Dashboard | Live map, wire feed and headline counts, with hazard overlays. |
| Earth Watch | NASA natural-event and disaster overlays on a map. |
| Conflict Watch | Conflict hotspots aggregated from the live wires. |
| Maritime | Shipping incidents plus the OFAC sanctioned-vessel registry. |
| Markets & Reserves | Global markets, yield curve and the crisis/stress engine. |
| Signals | Aircraft map, space weather and attention; aircraft watches. |
| Visual Intel | All photos, video and satellite imagery in one gallery. |
| Research | Live news search, verification and pinning. |
| Link Analysis | Pivot a domain / IP / email / name into connected infrastructure. |
| Sanctions Screening | Screen names and vessels against the OFAC lists. |
| Social Footprint | Gated username footprint across public profile sites. |
| Dark Web Search | Gated dark-web index search (never fetches onion pages). |
| Workbench | Search, timeline and organise findings into Cases; evidence export. |
| Briefs & Reports | Rapid Briefs and compiled, audited, sourced briefs. |
| Watchlists | Track terms and get in-app and pushed alerts. |
| Trends | Daily volume by topic, severity and place. |
| OSINT Tools | A curated toolkit plus built-in domain/IP recon. |
| Subject Lookup | Gated phone/username lookups (only if your provider enables it). |
| Settings | AI, profile, two-factor, text size and alert preferences. |

## Optional: connect your own AI

In **Settings → AI Connection** you can link your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini
account. AI buttons then appear to draft reports, summarise searches and explain
verification — always from the sources on your screen. It's optional, and
Calibrex works fully without it.

## Tips

- **Treat leads as leads.** Social posts are flagged unverified, and images can be old or recycled. Confirm before you publish.
- **Use Read, not just the headline.** The full article opens inside Calibrex.
- **Gated tools need an agreement.** Social Footprint and Dark Web Search are off until an admin enables them, and each use is logged.
- **Comfortable text is on** by default (Settings → Display); turn on larger text there too.
- **Works on your phone** — a bottom nav bar gives you Home, Research, Watch and Report; swipe left/right to move between screens.
- **Install it as an app** — from Settings, add Calibrex to your home screen for a full-screen PWA that updates itself.
