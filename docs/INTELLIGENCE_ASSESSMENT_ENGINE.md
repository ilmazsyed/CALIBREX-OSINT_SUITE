# Intelligence Assessment Engine

## Purpose
This engine turns case evidence into structured, reviewable analyst assessments across five roles: Defence Intelligence / Information Environment, Corporate Intelligence / Risk, Logistics / Supply Chain Risk, Geopolitical Intelligence, and Strategic Intelligence / Foresight.

## Assessment record
Each case can carry:
- Role lens
- Key judgements with analyst confidence, supporting and contradicting evidence references, assumptions, implications and review dates
- Explicit assumptions
- Alternative hypotheses with evidence for and against
- Indicators with baseline, operator, threshold, observed value, source evidence and trigger status
- Scenarios with likelihood band, impact band, triggers, assumptions and response options

Evidence references are validated against the case's evidence register. A judgement confidence is an analyst's stated confidence, not a calculated probability or model truth score. Indicators without observed values remain UNASSESSED. A trigger is a threshold comparison, not proof of an event.

## Assessment quality
The quality score measures record completeness only: key judgements, evidence linkage, alternatives, assumptions, indicators, scenarios, source diversity and evidence base. It is not a probability, accuracy rating, threat score or substitute for peer review. Distinct publisher domains do not prove editorial independence.

## Role-specific workflow
All roles use the same core cycle: define the decision question; collect source-linked observations; separate fact/claim/assessment/hypothesis/unknown; compare sources and contradictions; write key judgements; test alternatives; monitor indicators; record implications and review dates.

Role profiles supply starter domains and indicator prompts. They are analyst prompts, not live feeds. Defence profiles do not provide classified intelligence, ORBAT truth or military telemetry. Corporate profiles do not replace licensed beneficial ownership, PEP, litigation or credit databases. Supply-chain profiles do not create AIS, port, freight or shipment tracking. Geopolitical and strategic profiles do not produce validated forecasts without historical data and expert review.

## Release and validation limits
This module is deterministic and keyless. It does not add collection credentials, scrape restricted sources, infer intent, resolve identity or automate attribution. It is a structured assessment layer, not a 9.5/10 performance guarantee. Role scores must be earned through customer workflow trials, source coverage measurements, analyst acceptance and forecast/outcome backtesting.
