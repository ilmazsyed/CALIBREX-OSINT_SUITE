# Calibrex OSINT Studio — Overview

*A one-page summary for customers and stakeholders.*
*Shareable web version: see the Getting Started link in the app (Platform Info).*

## What it is

Calibrex OSINT Studio is a private, login-gated web platform for open-source
intelligence. It pulls live, public information from the web every five
minutes, scores it into a single risk picture across every monitor, maps it,
and gives analysts the tools to dig in, confirm, and write it up. Real sources
only — nothing simulated.

## What your team gets

| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Command** | A composite risk index plus a live band for every monitor, re-scoped in one tap between Global, India and World — the home screen and single risk picture. |
| **Live threat map** | Every located event as a marker, grouped by place, colour-coded by severity, with earthquake and disaster overlays. |
| **Earth, Conflict & Maritime** | NASA natural-event overlays, conflict hotspots aggregated from the wires, and maritime incidents cross-referenced with the OFAC sanctioned-vessel registry — each on its own live map. |
| **Research & Verify** | Live news search with a one-click check: how many independent outlets carry a story in the last three days. |
| **Link Analysis** | Pivot a domain, IP, email or name into its connected infrastructure on an interactive graph, from passive public sources. |
| **Sanctions screening** | Screen any name or vessel against the OFAC sanctions lists with exact, substring and fuzzy matching. |
| **Gated Social Footprint** | Username footprint across public profile sites — off by default, enabled by an admin, agreement-gated and logged on every use. |
| **Workbench & evidence export** | Timeline everything ingested, pin findings into persistent Cases, and export a court-ready report with per-item SHA-256 integrity hashes. |
| **Briefs & Reports** | Fire a Rapid Brief in seconds, or turn pinned reports into an editable, fully sourced brief and export it. |
| **Watchlists & alerts** | Save the names, places or groups you track and get told the moment they appear — in-app, or pushed to Telegram / a webhook. |
| **Markets & crisis engine** | Global indices, the US yield curve and commodities, with plain-language alerts for crashes, currency records and country stress. |
| **Signals & Trends** | Live aircraft (military highlighted), space weather, public attention, and daily volume by topic, severity and place. |
| **OSINT Tools** | A curated toolkit plus built-in domain/IP infrastructure recon. |

## How a story flows

1. **Scan** — Command gives one risk picture across every monitor, scoped Global, India or World.
2. **Spot** — an event surfaces on a live board's map or the wire feed, rated by severity.
3. **Read & see** — open the full article inside Calibrex, with its photos, video and satellite views.
4. **Verify** — check how many independent outlets carry the same story right now.
5. **Investigate** — pivot the infrastructure behind a site on the graph, screen names against sanctions, or dig with the OSINT toolkit.
6. **Report** — compile a sourced, hash-stamped brief, or fire a Rapid Brief, and hand it to your desk.

## Where the intelligence comes from

- **Wires & world news:** topic searches (South Asia terror, terror finance, regional security, power-axis, cyber, kinetic) plus BBC, Al Jazeera, DW, France 24.
- **Regional depth:** The Hindu, Times of India, Hindustan Times, NDTV, ThePrint, Greater Kashmir, PIB, India Today's OSINT team, Dawn, SATP.
- **Analysis & OSINT:** Bellingcat, Crisis Group, ACLED, CTC, Jamestown, plus vetted OSINT accounts on Bluesky and Telegram (always labelled unverified).
- **Ground truth:** NASA daily imagery, natural-event feeds and fire detections, USGS earthquakes, GDACS disaster alerts.
- **Registries & lists:** OFAC sanctions lists (names and vessels), passive infrastructure records (certificate transparency, DNS, RDAP, PTR) for Link Analysis.

Any source can be switched off, and admins can add their own feeds, Telegram
channels, Bluesky accounts or news searches.

## Built for control and trust

- **Approved access only.** New users stay pending until an administrator activates them. Accounts can be suspended or restored at any time.
- **Two-factor sign-in.** Users can enable TOTP two-factor authentication on their own account.
- **Verification, not assumption.** Three or more independent outlets marks a story corroborated. Every result carries its sources; social posts are flagged unverified. Observed reporting is labelled as such — never a forecast.
- **Auditable by design.** Sensitive tools (SOCMINT) are off by default, require an accepted usage agreement on every use, and are logged for the administrator to review. Profile edits are logged to the administrator too.

## Optional AI

Each user can connect their own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account to draft
reports and summarise findings — grounded only in the sources on screen, and
switchable off for the whole workspace. Calibrex works fully without it.
