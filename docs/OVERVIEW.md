# Calibrex OSINT Studio — Overview

*A one-page summary for customers and stakeholders.*
*Shareable web version: see the Getting Started link in the app (Platform Info).*

## What it is

Calibrex OSINT Studio is a private, login-gated web platform for open-source
intelligence. It pulls live, public information from the web every five
minutes, sorts it by threat and severity, maps it, and gives analysts the
tools to dig in, confirm, and write it up. Real sources only — nothing
simulated.

## What your team gets

| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Live threat map** | Every located event as a marker, grouped by place, colour-coded by severity, with earthquake and disaster overlays. |
| **Visual Intel** | Every photo, video and satellite view from the reporting in one gallery, with NASA imagery of each hot spot. |
| **Research & Verify** | Live news search with a one-click check: how many independent outlets carry a story in the last three days. |
| **Reports & dispatch** | Turn pinned reports into an editable, fully sourced brief and export it. |
| **Watchlists & alerts** | Save the names, places or groups you track and get told the moment they appear in the news. |
| **Trends** | Daily volume by topic, severity and place, and the locations getting more attention than usual. |
| **OSINT Tools** | A curated toolkit plus built-in domain/IP infrastructure recon. |

## How a story flows

1. **Spot** — an event surfaces on the map or the live wire feed, rated by severity.
2. **Read & see** — open the full article inside Calibrex, with its photos, video and satellite views.
3. **Verify** — check how many independent outlets carry the same story right now.
4. **Investigate** — trace the infrastructure behind a site, or dig with the OSINT toolkit.
5. **Report** — compile a sourced brief and hand it to your desk from Dispatch Studio.

## Where the intelligence comes from

- **Wires & world news:** six topic searches (South Asia terror, terror finance, regional security, power-axis, cyber, kinetic) plus BBC, Al Jazeera, DW, France 24.
- **Regional depth:** The Hindu, Times of India, Hindustan Times, NDTV, ThePrint, Greater Kashmir, PIB, India Today's OSINT team, Dawn, SATP.
- **Analysis & OSINT:** Bellingcat, Crisis Group, ACLED, CTC, Jamestown, plus vetted OSINT accounts on Bluesky and Telegram (always labelled unverified).
- **Ground truth:** NASA daily imagery and fire detections, USGS earthquakes, GDACS disaster alerts.

Any source can be switched off, and admins can add their own feeds, Telegram
channels, Bluesky accounts or news searches.

## Built for control and trust

- **Approved access only.** New users stay pending until an administrator activates them. Accounts can be suspended or restored at any time.
- **Verification, not assumption.** Three or more independent outlets marks a story corroborated. Every result carries its sources; social posts are flagged unverified.
- **Auditable by design.** Sensitive lookups are off by default, require an accepted usage agreement on every use, and are logged for the administrator to review.

## Optional AI

Each user can connect their own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account to draft
reports and summarise findings — grounded only in the sources on screen, and
switchable off for the whole workspace. Calibrex works fully without it.
