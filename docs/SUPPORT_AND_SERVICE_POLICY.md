# Support and Service Policy — Draft for Approval

This is an internal draft. Do not publish uptime guarantees or response-time commitments until monitoring, staffing and contractual approval support them.

## Support channels

Each paying customer should receive a named support contact and a documented support channel. Support requests must include a request ID, timestamp, affected workflow and impact summary. Do not request passwords, API keys or session cookies.

## Severity model

| Severity | Definition | Initial handling target (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Service unavailable or confirmed cross-customer data exposure | Immediate escalation during covered support hours |
| P2 High | Core collection, investigation or authentication workflow materially impaired | Same business day |
| P3 Normal | Non-critical feature defect or degraded source | Next business day |
| P4 Request | Question, enhancement or documentation request | Planned backlog review |

These are proposed handling targets, not contractual SLAs.

## Incident handling

1. Record the incident, affected version, start time and request IDs.
2. Contain access or collection issues; preserve relevant logs.
3. Assess customer data impact and legal notification obligations.
4. Communicate verified facts, not speculation.
5. Restore service using the documented rollback or recovery procedure.
6. Record root cause, corrective actions, owners and deadlines.

## Availability and maintenance

Do not advertise an uptime percentage until production monitoring has accumulated sufficient data and exclusions, measurement windows and remedies are contractually defined.

## Customer offboarding

Confirm authorised requester, export scope, retention exceptions, deletion completion and account/session revocation. Retain only records required by contract or applicable law.
