# Investigation evidence map

The Investigation Studio includes a case-specific map of recorded evidence coordinates.

- Only coordinates stored on evidence records are plotted.
- Marker colours follow evidence assessment: FACT, CLAIM, ASSESSMENT, HYPOTHESIS and UNKNOWN.
- Reported accuracy radii are drawn where available and within the map display limit.
- Popups show the evidence title, classification, triage, coordinates, reported accuracy, saved provider context, location method and source link.
- The map fits to the evidence extent and does not assume an India-only area.

No lexical headline candidates or inferred gazetteer coordinates are plotted. Markers are evidence observations, not confirmed event locations. Source-reported accuracy radii are not independently verified.
